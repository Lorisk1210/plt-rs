The AFM font metrics of crates/pltrs-font/fonts/afm
===================================================

pltrs ships the 47 Adobe Font Metrics files matplotlib ships in
mpl-data/fonts/afm, unmodified, beside the readme.txt carrying Adobe's
notice (crates/pltrs-font/fonts/afm/readme.txt). They describe the fonts;
they hold no glyph outlines.

Adobe Systems' files
--------------------

The metrics of the 35 standard PostScript fonts:

  pagd8a.afm pagdo8a.afm pagk8a.afm pagko8a.afm      ITC Avant Garde Gothic
  pbkd8a.afm pbkdi8a.afm pbkl8a.afm pbkli8a.afm      ITC Bookman
  pcrb8a.afm pcrbo8a.afm pcrr8a.afm pcrro8a.afm      Courier
  phvb8a.afm phvbo8a.afm phvr8a.afm phvro8a.afm      Helvetica
  phvb8an.afm phvbo8an.afm phvr8an.afm phvro8an.afm  Helvetica Narrow
  pncb8a.afm pncbi8a.afm pncr8a.afm pncri8a.afm      New Century Schoolbook
  pplb8a.afm pplbi8a.afm pplr8a.afm pplri8a.afm      Palatino
  psyr.afm                                           Symbol
  ptmb8a.afm ptmbi8a.afm ptmr8a.afm ptmri8a.afm      Times
  pzcmi8a.afm                                        ITC Zapf Chancery

The metrics of Helvetica Light (phvl8a.afm, phvlo8a.afm) and of Utopia
(putb8a.afm, putbi8a.afm, putr8a.afm, putri8a.afm) are Adobe's files too,
with Adobe's copyright notice, as matplotlib distributes them.
