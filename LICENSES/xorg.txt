X.Org's keysym definitions and keysym-to-Unicode table

pltrs-platform's keysym tables (crates/pltrs-platform/src/keysym) are
generated from X.Org's keysymdef.h and XF86keysym.h (xorgproto 2024.1)
and take libX11's keysym-to-UCS mapping (KeySymToUcs4). Their notices
follow.
