Font Metrics for the Standard PostScript Fonts
==============================================

This directory holds the 47 AFM files of matplotlib's mpl-data/fonts/afm,
copied unmodified from matplotlib 3.11.2. The title and these first two
paragraphs were added by the pltrs developers.

The 41 files by Adobe Systems are the metrics of the 35 standard PostScript
fonts (ITC Avant Garde Gothic, ITC Bookman, Courier, Helvetica and Helvetica
Narrow, New Century Schoolbook, Palatino, Symbol, Times, ITC Zapf Chancery
and ITC Zapf Dingbats), of Helvetica Light and of Utopia; each keeps Adobe's
copyright notice. Adobe distributes the metrics of the 35 standard fonts with
the following notice. The six Computer Modern files (cm*.afm) are by the
American Mathematical Society and by Basil K. Malyshev (cmti10.afm, the
BaKoMa Fonts Collection); see LICENSES/adobe-afm.txt in the pltrs sources.

This file and the 35 PostScript(R) AFM files it accompanies may be used, copied,
and distributed for any purpose and without charge, with or without modification,
provided that all copyright notices are retained; that the AFM files are not
distributed without this file; that all modifications to this file or any of
the AFM files are prominently noted in the modified file(s); and that this
paragraph is not modified. Adobe Systems has no responsibility or obligation
to support the use of the AFM files.
