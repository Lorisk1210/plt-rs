# matplotlib draws what a program puts in a figure's artists, lines, patches
# and texts lists (Figure.get_children reads them at each draw); pltrs
# (Rust) keeps the figure's artists, so these lists are a list subclass
# whose mutating methods hand the artists over (isinstance(fig.lines, list)
# holds, and they compare equal to matplotlib's lists).
artists/(sub)?figure_(getters|setters)$ | ^type\[(artists|lines|patches|texts)\]: list -> matplotlib\._figure_artists\.DrawnList$ | the figure's lists of loose artists are a list subclass that has pltrs (Rust) draw what is put in them

# matplotlib's Artist.set_mouseover adds the artist to self.axes._mouseover_set;
# the axes of a (sub)figure is the list of its Axes, so a figure with Axes
# fails there with AttributeError (a matplotlib bug), where pltrs sets it.
artists/subfigure_setters$ | ^exception\[set_mouseover\]: missing AttributeError$ | matplotlib fails reading _mouseover_set from the list a SubFigure's axes attribute is
api/(figure_[0-9]+|subfigure_0)$ | ^exception\[(set_mouseover|set|constant)\]: missing AttributeError$ | matplotlib fails reading _mouseover_set from the list a (Sub)Figure's axes attribute is: set_mouseover(True), mouseover = True and set(mouseover=True)

# pltrs (Rust) lays out the tree of an added offset box itself and asks a
# box of the program's in it for its extent once per layout (its
# get_bbox, through measure_box), then draws it once, where matplotlib's
# parents ask a child's get_bbox again in each of their own get_bbox,
# get_offset and draw (an AnchoredOffsetbox three times per draw); the
# extents, offsets and images are the same.
generated/offsetboxes/program_boxes$ | ^callback\[[^]]+\]: runs another number of times$ | a box of the program's inside a box pltrs (Rust) lays out is measured once per layout, where matplotlib's parents measure their children again in their get_bbox, get_offset and draw
