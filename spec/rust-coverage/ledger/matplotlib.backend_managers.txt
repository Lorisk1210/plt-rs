## ToolManager
ToolManager -> pltrs::figure::Figure::add_tool, pltrs::figure::Figure::tools, pltrs::figure::Figure::trigger_tool
ToolManager.canvas n/a the canvas is part of the figure
ToolManager.figure n/a the figure is the receiver of the tool methods
ToolManager.set_figure n/a artists are made in their figure; Axes::attach / Figure::attach move them
ToolManager.toolmanager_connect -> pltrs::figure::Figure::on_tool_change
ToolManager.toolmanager_disconnect -> pltrs::figure::Figure::disconnect_tool_change
ToolManager.message_event -> pltrs::figure::Figure::tool_message
ToolManager.active_toggle -> pltrs::figure::Figure::active_toggle
ToolManager.get_tool_keymap -> pltrs::figure::Figure::tool
ToolManager.update_keymap -> pltrs::figure::Figure::set_tool_keys
ToolManager.remove_tool -> pltrs::figure::Figure::remove_tool
